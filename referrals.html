Commercial referrals and editorial independence

Parking Charge Helper is an independent information website operated by Kevin Harris. We may eventually earn commission from qualifying bookings via referral links. Currently there are no active affiliate agreements or commission-generating links on this site.

If an affiliate relationship is introduced, the relevant recommendation will be labelled. Referral arrangements will not affect our obligation to present accurate information or to distinguish free parking appeals from paid convenience services.

We do not sell legal representation or promise to cancel parking charges.
